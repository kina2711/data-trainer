# MADR templates — hồ sơ nguồn

## Phạm vi đã đọc

- ADR core: status, context/problem, considered options, decision outcome and consequences.
- MADR additions: decision drivers, pros/cons, confirmation and explicit rationale.

## Giới hạn

Template structures a decision; it does not supply workload evidence or approve the decision. L216 adds thresholds, measurements and reversal triggers as curriculum synthesis.
